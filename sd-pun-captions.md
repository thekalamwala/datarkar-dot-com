## How to post (per image, your pace)
1. Facebook → your Page → **Photo/Video** → upload the PNG → paste caption → **Post**.
2. Instagram → **+** → upload same PNG → paste caption → **Share**. (Phone app easiest; web also posts single images.)
3. Repeat next image when ready.
